What was taken, what was not taken, and the four steps to take today.

Someone accessed your personal data at Fernway

What happened: An unauthorised party accessed a database backup held by
one of our suppliers. It started on 2 September 2026. We discovered it
on 5 September 2026. Your Fernway account is one of the accounts it
reached, and some of your personal data was in it.

What was and was not involved

Data involved: Name, work email address, and hashed password
Data not involved: Payment card numbers, bank details, and customer site addresses

What we list as not involved is not a guess. It is the data we have
checked and confirmed stayed out of this breach. If that changes, or
if we find that more was involved, we will send you another email
and say so on the breach updates page.

What to do now

1. Change your Fernway password. The link below opens the
page where you set a new one.

2. If you used that same password on any other site, change it on
those sites as well.

3. Turn on two-step verification for your Fernway account,
so that a password on its own is not enough to sign in.

4. Read through your recent account activity, and write to us about
anything you do not recognise.

Important: Fake messages follow a breach, and they use the stolen

What we have done

We closed the way in on 5 September 2026. We ended every
signed-in session on the accounts that were reached, so a stolen
session cannot be used. An outside security firm is now going
through our systems and our logs with us, and we have reported this
breach to the data protection authority that regulates us.

That work is not finished. We will publish what we learn, including
anything that makes this worse than it looks today.

Your data was in our care and we did not keep it safe. That is our
failure, and we are sorry for it.
