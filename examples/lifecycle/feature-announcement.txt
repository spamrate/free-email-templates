Saved routes is now in Fernway

A run of jobs you order once and reuse every week. It replaces
rebuilding the same route every Monday morning. It is switched on for
everyone in Brightpath Mechanical with the Dispatcher role, so there is
nothing to turn on, and nothing you have already set up changes.

Three things it now makes possible:

- Keep a standing route for each crew and swap jobs in and out
- Hand a route to a stand-in without explaining the order
- Reuse last week without setting it up again
