Scheduled maintenance for Fernway

Fernway will be offline for planned maintenance from 02:00 UTC on 14
September 2026 to 04:00 UTC on 14 September 2026. Both times are given
with their timezone. If that is not your own timezone, convert them
before you plan around the window.

Maintenance window

Starts: 02:00 UTC on 14 September 2026
Ends: 04:00 UTC on 14 September 2026
Duration: up to 2 hours
Affected: Job dispatch and the mobile app

The window above is the maximum, not the plan. The work often finishes
sooner, and Fernway comes back as soon as it is done. Nothing waits for
04:00 UTC on 14 September 2026 once the work is over.

What stops during the window

- Sign-in and the web app return an error.
- The API returns an error on every request.
- Scheduled jobs and webhooks are held, then run after the window
closes.

What keeps working

- Your data stays where it is. Nothing is deleted or changed.
- Work you saved before 02:00 UTC on 14 September 2026 is there when the
window closes.
- The status page stays up for the whole window.

Before 02:00 UTC on 14 September 2026, finish any long-running job or
postpone it until after 04:00 UTC on 14 September 2026. An export, an
import or a bulk edit that is still going when the window opens will
fail part way through.

If your own software calls the API, expect errors for the length of
the window and let it retry afterwards, rather than treating the
failure as final.
